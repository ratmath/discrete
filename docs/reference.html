Reference

Everything in the library

Every export of @ratmath/discrete. Whole-number arguments may be numbers, BigInts or digit strings; counts and ranks come back as BigInts.

Counting

ExportWhat it does
factorial(n), doubleFactorial(n)n!n!n! and n!!n!!n!!.
fallingFactorial(n, k), risingFactorial(n, k)n(n−1)⋯(n−k+1)n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)n(n−1)⋯(n−k+1) and n(n+1)⋯(n+k−1)n(n+1)\cdots(n+k-1)n(n+1)⋯(n+k−1); n may be negative.
binomial(n, k)(nk)\binom nk(kn​); 0 outside the triangle; the series coefficient for negative n.
multinomial(k1, k2, …)(k1+⋯+kr)!/(k1!⋯kr!)(k_1 + \dots + k_r)!/(k_1!\cdots k_r!)(k1​+⋯+kr​)!/(k1​!⋯kr​!); also takes one array.
multichoose(n, k)Multisets of size k from n kinds.
pascalRow(n)Row n of Pascal's triangle.
stirling2(n, k), bell(n), surjections(n, k)Set partitions into k blocks, all set partitions, functions onto a k-set.
stirling1(n, k, { signed })Permutations with k cycles; signed gives the coefficients of the falling factorial.
catalan(n), fibonacci(n), lucas(n)Fibonacci and Lucas accept negative and very large n.
derangementCount(n), eulerian(n, k)Permutations with no fixed point; with exactly k descents.
partitionCount(n), partitionCount(n, k)Partitions of the integer n; into exactly k parts.
compositionCount(n), compositionCount(n, k)Ordered sums of positive parts.

Principles

ExportWhat it does
inclusionExclusion(sets, { universe }){ union, none, terms } for arrays or Sets; terms[j] has size, sign, sum, count.
sieve(m, sizeOf)The sieve formula for m symmetric properties: { all, none, union, terms }.
pigeonhole(items, holes){ atLeast, atMost, forced, statement }.
collision(items, key)The first two items with the same key: { key, items, indices } or null.

Families

ExportWhat it does
Familycount (BigInt), unrank(i) / at(i), rank(object), has(object), random(rng or seed), first(n), slice(from, to), toArray(), iteration with for…of, name, order.
permutations(n or items, k)Arrangements of all, or of k, in lexicographic order.
derangements(n or items)Permutations with no fixed point.
combinations(n or items, k)k-element subsets.
multisets(n or items, k)Selections with repetition.
subsets(n or items, { order })All subsets; order "binary", "gray" or "size".
words(n or letters, k)k-letter words; rank also accepts a string.
product(a, b, …)The Cartesian product of arrays and families.
compositions(n, k, { zero })Ordered sums; with k parts; with zero parts allowed.
partitions(n, k)Integer partitions, largest part first.
setPartitions(n or items, k)Partitions of a set into blocks; exactly k blocks.
dyckWords(n, { open, close })Balanced bracket strings.
